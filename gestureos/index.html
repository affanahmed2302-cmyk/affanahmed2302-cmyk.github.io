GestureOS

Touchless PC control with hand gestures. Webcam in → mouse, clicks, media & window actions out. Built with Python, MediaPipe & OpenCV.

PythonMediaPipeOpenCVPyAutoGUIRealtime CV

🖱️ Cursor & clicks

Index tracking for mouse move. Pinch for click. Fist for drag mode.

🔊 Media control

Palm play/pause, peace mute, thumbs for volume — no keyboard needed.

🪟 Window actions

Gestures for desktop, maximize, and tab switching.

📹 Webcam only

No special hardware — consumer camera + computer vision pipeline.

Gesture simulator

Tap a gesture — see what the system would do (UI demo; full stack runs locally with your webcam).
